# Igbo Mastery — Marketing Strategy Playbook
### ₦0 budget. Everything free. Built for a diaspora audience.

---

## 1. Who we are selling to (and the words that reach them)

Our customer is not a "language learner" in the abstract. Our customer is:

1. **A parent abroad** (US, UK, Canada, also Germany, Ireland, Australia) whose child
   understands Igbo but answers in English — and who feels that loss.
2. **A heritage adult** who understands some Igbo, cannot speak it, and is tired of
   feeling like a stranger at family gatherings.
3. **A grandparent or auntie** in the family WhatsApp group who keeps saying
   "ụmụ anyị amaghị asụsụ" — our children don't know the language.

**The single strongest message:** "Your child will greet their grandmother in Igbo."
That beats every "learn a new skill" message. We are not selling a course. We are
selling connection, identity, and the rescue of something the family is scared of losing.

**Words that work:** roots, heritage, grandmother, home, identity, "bring it home",
"speak to your elders", "your name, said properly", "before it's too late".
**Words to avoid:** "cheap", "course material", "study", "curriculum", "students".

---

## 2. The three tiers, said in one line each

| Tier | Price | One-line pitch |
|---|---|---|
| Free Foundation | ₦0 | "Learn the tones, the alphabet and the dotted letters — genuinely free." |
| Survival Igbo | ₦500 once | "Greet, count, talk family, order food, bargain in the market." |
| Igbo Fluency Kit | ₦2,000 once | "Flashcards, writing practice, dialogues, proverbs — and a certificate." |

Always say **"one time, no subscription"**. Diaspora customers are subscription-tired.

---

## 3. Where our audience actually is (free channels, in priority order)

1. **WhatsApp** — the #1 channel for this audience. Family groups, Igbo community
   groups, church groups, association groups (Igbo Union USA/UK/Canada chapters,
   Ohanaeze affiliate groups). This is where trust lives.
2. **Facebook** — Igbo diaspora groups are large and active. Also where the
   35–60 age bracket (the parents) spends time.
3. **Instagram & TikTok** — where the heritage learners (18–35) are. Our vertical
   videos are built for this.
4. **YouTube** — long-term home for the full video lessons and shorts.
5. **Reddit** — r/Nigeria, r/igbo, r/NigerianFluency and similar. Answer questions
   genuinely; never hard-sell.
6. **Email/WhatsApp broadcast list** — our own list of people who signed up for the
   free course. This is the asset that matters most.

---

## 4. The ₦0 launch plan (first 30 days)

**Week 1 — Plant the flag**
- Create the accounts: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Page, YouTube channel. Same name
  everywhere: **Igbo Mastery**. Use the bios in PROFILE-BIOS.md.
- Post the profile photo + banner (use the feed posters).
- Post video 1 (the akwa quartet) on all three platforms. Pin it.
- Join 10 WhatsApp/Facebook Igbo groups. Lurk for 2 days, learn the tone of each
  group. Do not post yet.

**Week 2 — Give value, ask for nothing**
- Post 3 videos (tones, dotted letters, greetings).
- Share the free cheat sheet in 3 groups with one line: "Made this for anyone
  teaching their kids Igbo — free, no signup." (See CHEAT-SHEET.pdf.)
- Answer every comment and DM within a few hours. This is free marketing.

**Week 3 — Soft launch the paid tiers**
- Post the pricing poster.
- Post 3 more videos (numbers, family, food).
- In groups you are now a familiar face in, share the site with a personal note:
  "I built a free Igbo foundation course for diaspora families — the tones part is
  the bit I wish I'd had growing up. Link in comments."
- Send WhatsApp message #1 from WHATSAPP-MESSAGES.md to your own contacts.

**Week 4 — Social proof loop**
- Ask your first 5 free users (friends, family — be honest, they count) for one
  sentence about why they are learning. Screenshot with permission. Post one
  "Why I'm learning Igbo" story set.
- Post 4 videos (market, daily life, 2 proverbs).
- Post the "who this is for" video (v18) — it converts lurkers.

**Daily rhythm after week 4:** 1 video/day (we have 20), 1 story (poll or
"word of the day"), answer everything. Post in 2 groups per week with value first.

## 5. The free-tier → paid conversion (our engine)

1. Free Foundation teaches something real (tones, alphabet, dotted letters). It ends
   with a natural wall: "Survival Igbo teaches greetings, numbers, family, food, the
   market and daily life."
2. Every free lesson page shows the next paid module, greyed with a lock.
3. WhatsApp message #7 (in WHATSAPP-MESSAGES.md) goes to anyone who signs up free
   and has not upgraded after 3 days — one message, no pressure.
4. The certificate is the emotional closer for the ₦2,000 tier: "Finish everything
   and earn a certificate with your name on it." Parents love this for their kids.

---

## 6. WhatsApp group etiquette (read before posting anywhere)

- **Never drop a link and leave.** Write 2–3 sentences of real value first.
- **Never post in more than 2–3 groups on the same day.** You will be removed as spam.
- **Answer questions in the group publicly** — it advertises your expertise for free.
- **Respect group rules.** Some groups ban all promotion; in those, just be helpful
  and let your profile bio carry the link.
- If an admin asks you to stop, stop immediately and thank them.

---

## 7. Partnerships worth chasing (all free)

- **Igbo associations & unions** (US/UK/Canada chapters) — offer a free "Igbo for
  the diaspora family" talk on Zoom. This is how you get 50 signups in one evening.
- **Churches with Igbo congregations** — offer the free foundation as a Sunday
  school resource.
- **Igbo teachers and tutors** (there are active ones on Zoom) — offer them a free
  Fluency Kit in exchange for honest feedback and a mention.
- **Nollywood/Igbo music fan pages** — comment genuinely on their posts; our best
  cultural hook is "understand the Igbo in your favourite films".
- **University African studies departments** — send a polite email offering the free
  foundation as a supplementary resource for students.

---

## 8. What to measure (keep it simple)

Weekly, in a notebook:
- New free accounts (the only number that matters early)
- Sales of ₦500 and ₦2,000 tiers
- Which video got the most saves/shares — make more like it
- Which WhatsApp message got replies

Do not chase followers. 300 free signups from the right people beats 10,000
random followers.

---

## 9. The 30-second version (if you only do five things)

1. Post one video every day, starting with the akwa quartet.
2. Share the free cheat sheet in Igbo WhatsApp and Facebook groups, with a personal line.
3. Reply to every single comment and DM.
4. Offer one free Zoom talk to an Igbo association.
5. Send WhatsApp message #1 to your own contacts this week.
