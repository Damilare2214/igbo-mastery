Each script lists exactly what appears on screen, in order. Tone-marked words
(ákwá, àkwá…) are shown in **gold** on screen; plain words in white. Videos are
20–26 seconds. Post one per day.

## Recipe for any future video (same every time)

1. Background: dark, African-textile inspired, no people, no text in the image.
2. Scrim: dark gradient top and bottom so text is always readable.
3. Movement: slow zoom in (Ken Burns), never pan.
4. Captions: big; gold for any word with a tone mark or dotted letter, white otherwise.
5. Pace: one idea every 3 seconds. Never more than 6 words on screen at once.
6. End every video with "link in bio" and the handle @igbo.mastery.
